# Lab Assistant's Toolkit — ZQM Labs


Everything an organizer ("Lab Assistant") needs to run a **ZQM Labs — Z's Quests & Mischief** scavenger-hunt game in their own space. *Games, gear & a little mischief.*


## Start here


1. [`guides/setup-checklist.md`](guides/setup-checklist.md) — the week-by-week countdown: what to do 2 weeks out, 1 week out, the day before, and day-of.
2. [`guides/print-specs.md`](guides/print-specs.md) — how to print the QR signs so they scan in sunlight, rain, and low light.
3. [`guides/day-of-troubleshooting.md`](guides/day-of-troubleshooting.md) — QR won't scan, GPS drift, ties, rain plan. Read once before the event; keep on your phone during it.
4. [`faq.md`](faq.md) — the questions organizers actually ask.


## The 60-second version


You get a kit: 6 QR signs + a start poster, a placement map, an answer key, and
a link players open on their phones (no app). You print the signs, place them,
pick a scare level on the Dial (Cozy / Spooky / After Dark / Overdrive), and the game runs
itself. One setup call with us before the event is included in every kit.
