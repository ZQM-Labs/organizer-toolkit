# Setup Checklist — running your ZQM Labs game

## 2 weeks out

- [ ] Pick your date, start time, and rain date.
- [ ] Pick your Dial setting: **Cozy** (all ages), **Spooky**
  (teens+), **After Dark** (adults), **Overdrive** (competitive
  offices).
- [ ] Walk your space and choose 6 waypoints, 2–4 minutes apart
  on foot. Note anything that could move or vanish (parked cars,
  decorations).
- [ ] Check the kit's six generic stops against your waypoints —
  the kit is not rewritten for your space.

## 1 week out

- [ ] Print the signs — see [print-specs.md](print-specs.md).
  Laminate or sleeve anything outdoors.
- [ ] Charge a backup phone battery; you'll keep the
  troubleshooting guide open on yours.
- [ ] Tell players: "Bring a phone, wear walking shoes."
  That's the whole packing list.

## Day before

- [ ] Place the signs. Tape/stake at eye level, QR facing the approach path.
  Keep signs out of tree-canopy shade — dappled or shaded signs scan
  slowly. If a stop is under trees, raise the sign to about 5.5 ft or
  shift it ~2 ft into even light / better sun exposure.
- [ ] Walk the full route in order, scanning every QR yourself, in the
  same light you'll run in. Fix anything that doesn't scan instantly.
- [ ] Check the forecast; decide by morning whether the rain plan (below) activates.

## Day of

- [ ] 1 hour before: re-walk the route. Signs wander; wind is the usual suspect.
- [ ] Gather players at the start poster. Read the 30-second
  briefing: "Six stops, scan the QR at each, solve the puzzle,
  hints cost you nothing but pride. Most solved wins."
- [ ] Start the clock. Roam, don't hover — the game runs itself.
- [ ] Finale: gather at the last stop, tally solved counts, award
  the tiebreaker if needed (your answer key has one), take the group
  photo.

## Rain plan

Light rain: play on — laminated signs survive. Heavy rain/storms:
postpone to the rain date. Never place signs where players must
cross traffic to reach them, wet or dry.
