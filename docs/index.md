# Lab Assistant's Toolkit

Everything an organizer ("Lab Assistant") needs to run a
**ZQM Labs — Z's Quests & Mischief** scavenger-hunt game in their own
space. *Games, gear & a little mischief.*

This is the day-of operations kit for organizers: the setup checklist, QR
sign generation guidance, print specs, day-of troubleshooting guide, and
FAQ. Game content itself lives in quest-packs; field failures and
adaptations found using this toolkit are recorded in playtests.

## Start here

1. [`guides/setup-checklist.md`](guides/setup-checklist.md) — the week-by-week countdown: what to do 2 weeks out, 1 week out, the day before, and day-of.
2. [`guides/qr-generator.md`](guides/qr-generator.md) — generate, test, and print QR signs for your kit (uses the in-repo [`qr-kit/`](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/organizer-toolkit/tree/main/qr-kit) generator).
3. [`guides/print-specs.md`](guides/print-specs.md) — how to print the QR signs so they scan in sunlight, rain, and low light.
4. [`guides/day-of-troubleshooting.md`](guides/day-of-troubleshooting.md) — QR won't scan, GPS drift, ties, rain plan. Read once before the event; keep on your phone during it.
5. [`faq.md`](faq.md) — the questions organizers actually ask.

## The 60-second version

You get a kit: 6 QR signs + a start poster, a placement map, an answer key,
and a link players open on their phones (no app). You print the signs,
place them, pick a scare level on the Dial (Cozy / Spooky / After Dark /
Overdrive), and the game runs itself. Every kit is the same generic kit — no setup call, no customized stories or puzzles.
