# Safe group challenges on a shared six-stop route

**Status: host playbook only, not a deployed real-time multiplayer feature.**

The current ZQM Labs quest pattern is one phone per team on an approved six-stop route. A host can run low-friction **asynchronous** group activities around it without changing quest-player code, pricing, checkout or player authorization.

## Three low-risk variants

1. **Cooperative teams.** Small teams complete the same approved route in a scheduled window; recognize completion and good collaboration, not foot speed.
2. **Observation reflection.** After the route, teams fill an optional paper bingo/reflection sheet. The host reviews it without collecting GPS histories, private camera rolls, or participant details.
3. **Staggered starts.** Start teams at agreed intervals to avoid queues. Timings are scheduling tools, not incentives to race across roads or crowds.

## Hard boundaries

- No real-time pursuit, GPS hide-and-seek, public coordinates, player stalking, persistent territory tracking, unsafe timed racing, or money-based hunts.
- Venue permission, crossings, heat/weather, adult supervision for minors, accessible alternatives, incident response and safe stopping criteria must be reviewed before any field session.
- Verify QR/manual fallback, hint use and offline reliability on the exact edition. Do not invent score totals: use the existing [tiebreakers and scoring guidance](tiebreakers-and-scoring.md) only where the target player rules match.
- Do not call a shared start-time activity "live multiplayer" or a copied score a verified friend duel.

## Before a future product feature

Private session identifiers, secure room join, anti-abuse moderation, accessibility, network/device fallback and consent-based score retention require their own design and testing. The six-stop validator must not be weakened to implement different game formats. Record dated playtests in [ZQM Labs Playtests](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/playtests).
