# Civic and tourism six-stop quest — pilot planning packet

**Status: prospective design template only.** This document is not a bookable standardized city product, approved route, partnership, certification, or proof of regional availability.

ZQM Labs' existing six-stop QR/GPS quest format could be adapted for civic storytelling, neighborhood pride, museum/visitor programming and team-building after coordination. No ready-made routes for arbitrary cities are claimed.

## Pilot audiences

| Audience | Intended outcome | Essential review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tourism / downtown | Welcoming self-guided discovery | Municipal rules, public-space use, heritage fact checking |
| Civic / neighborhood | Resident stories and place-based learning | Community authorship, accessibility, no sensitive addresses |
| School / museum | Supervised learning | Age suitability, supervision, approvals, indoor alternatives |
| Corporate / nonprofit | Cooperative team experience | Venue consent, group size, weather plan, no unsafe timed race |

## Six-stop editorial canvas (NOT a validated pack)

| Stop | Editorial purpose | Illustrative topic; NOT a physical route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Welcome and orientation | Fictional town founding |
| 2 | Time and change | Two properly licensed historical photographs |
| 3 | Local creativity | Public art with artist attribution |
| 4 | Environment | Non-sensitive interpretive ecology |
| 5 | Community voices | Consented quotation plus non-photo alternative |
| 6 | Shared finale | Cooperative recap, not a footrace |

For each stop, document educational objective, a public or permissioned venue, original story, observation puzzle, three graded hints, QR/manual fallback, and an accessible alternative. Coordinate six lawful stops only after route review. Do not publish private homes, participant location histories, or sensitive-site details.

## Host discovery and intake fields

1. Organization and private contact; city and venue type; planned dates and hours.
2. Audience, team count, age bands, supervision, language and accessibility needs.
3. Learning objective, original/cleared imagery, historian or local-review contact.
4. Venue permissions, sign placement rules, safe crossings, heat and weather plan.
5. Content ownership and redistribution permissions for any supplied materials.
6. Desired scope: assess existing content, commission a custom story, or inquire about future white-label licensing. **Availability, fees and deliverables require explicit agreement**.
7. Success measures: completed teams, anonymous feedback, support incidents and qualified organizer follow-up, collected with appropriate consent.

Never request precise GPS histories, students' names, home addresses or private camera rolls.

## Review-to-delivery gates

- Discovery and feasibility: verify intended dates, venue jurisdiction, audience, rights, staffing, accessibility and budget. Do not claim instant purchase.
- Design: confirm six safe stops, original/cleared source material and three-hint ladders, plus a non-GPS fallback where supported.
- Technical: prepare a canonical SPEC v1 JSON; run the quest-authoring validator and target-player compatibility tests. This template alone is not a playable pack.
- Field: verify the route on site, with lawful signage, weather/heat plans, offline and QR recovery, accessibility, and participant safety.
- Evidence: use [ZQM Labs Playtests](https://github.com/ZQM-Labs/playtests) for dated, commit-specific outcomes.
- Commercial: independently verify booking, quote, checkout and fulfillment before describing the new offering as orderable.
- Publication: create city-specific pages only after a validated local story, actual permissions, and delivery capability exist. Avoid duplicate "[city]" doorway pages.
