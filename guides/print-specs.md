# Print Specs — QR signs that scan

The kit generator emits Letter-size (8.5×11") sign HTML. These specs are what
make the difference between "scanned instantly" and "ten people crowding a sign."

## Paper & protection

- **Outdoor:** print on cardstock and laminate, or slip into sheet protectors
  sealed with tape at the top. Unprotected paper dies in Florida humidity.
- **Indoor:** plain paper is fine; mount on foam board if signs will stand alone.

## Size & placement

- QR code prints at ~4.2". Don't shrink it — phone cameras need
  the size at arm's length.
- Mount at **eye level** (about 5 ft), QR facing the direction
  players approach from.
- One sign per stop, plus the start poster at the gathering point.

## Light

- **Avoid direct sun glare** on laminated signs: angle the sign slightly
  downward or place it in open shade. Glare is the #1 scan failure.
- After dark: place signs near existing light (porch lights, path lights)
  or clip a $1 LED tea light nearby. Test with your own phone at night.

## Test protocol

After placing all signs, walk the route in order and scan every QR with your
phone. If any code takes more than 2 seconds to scan, move or re-angle the sign.
This 15-minute walk prevents 100% of day-of QR emergencies.
