# Organizer FAQ

**Do players need to install an app?**
No. The player page runs in the phone browser and works offline once loaded.

**How many players?**
6–60. Teams of 3–5 work best; the kit scales by printing nothing extra — teams
share one phone per team.

**How long does it take?**
30–60 minutes for 6 stops, depending on walking distance and how much teams
argue about riddles (they will).

**What ages?**
The Dial sets it: Cozy is all-ages, Spooky suits teens+, After Dark is for
adults, and Overdrive is the competitive office setting.

**What if our space is small?**
A backyard works. Stops can be 20 feet apart — the puzzles carry the game,
not the hiking.

**Can we customize the quest text?**
No. Flat kits are generic — the same stories and puzzles for every buyer, not written around your space, and there is no setup call to customize them. Choose waypoints that fit the kit's six stops as closely as your space allows.

**Who sees our photos/scores?**
Only you, unless you share them with us for the community showcase (with
permission, always).

**What does the kit cost?**
Community tier $199 · Office tier $349–499. One flat fee, yours to replay
for your group during the event window.
